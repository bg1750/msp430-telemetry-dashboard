MSP430 Telemetry

I had a MSP430 microcontroller from college that I decided to repurpose as a personal project: the MSP430 takes its own temperature and supply voltage and sends them up the serial wire to this page, once every half second.

This project aimed to demonstrate skills in C, Python, and web development.
